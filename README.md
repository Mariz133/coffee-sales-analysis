# Coffee Sales Analysis

## Project Information
- **Course:** MAT 327 - Statistical Inference
- **Project:** Exploratory Data Analysis
- **Dataset:** Coffee Sales Dataset
- **Dataset Creator:** Navjot Kaushal

## Project Description
This project explores a coffee sales dataset to understand coffee purchasing patterns, purchase times, and transaction amounts. I selected this dataset because of my experience working in a café and my interest in understanding customer purchasing behavior.

## Research Questions
1. Which type of coffee is purchased most often?
2. What time of day has the most coffee purchases?
3. How do transaction amounts vary, and is there a relationship between purchase hour and transaction amount?

## Analysis
The project includes:
- Bar chart showing coffee purchases by type
- Histogram showing the distribution of purchase hours
- Histogram showing the distribution of transaction amounts
- Bar chart showing purchases by time of day
- Scatterplot comparing purchase hour and transaction amount
- Summary statistics, modes, and correlation analysis

## Main Findings
- Americano with Milk was the most frequently purchased coffee type.
- 10 AM had the highest number of purchases among the recorded hours.
- The mean transaction amount was approximately 31.65, and the median was 32.82.
- Afternoon had the highest number of purchases among the three time-of-day categories.
- The correlation between purchase hour and transaction amount was approximately 0.203, indicating a weak positive relationship.
